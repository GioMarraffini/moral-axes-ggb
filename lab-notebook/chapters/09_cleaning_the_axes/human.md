# 9. Can the harm control be separated from the IH axis?

## The question

Chapter 8 found that raising IH with the answer-built direction raises acceptance of harm
with no benefit by about the same amount. Is that a second thing the direction carries,
removable by projection, or is it the same thing?

## What was done

From the control answers of chapter 8 (704 accepting and 1,830 rejecting answers on
pointless harm, 2,526 and 268 on kindness, 745 and 379 on neutral opinions) three more
accepting-minus-rejecting directions were built the same way as the IB and IH ones. Then
the IH direction was steered with the pointless-harm direction projected out, and with
pointless harm and kindness projected out; the IB direction with kindness (and neutral
agreement) projected out. Chain of thought on scales and controls, three iterations.

## Result

Cosines at layer 23: IH with pointless harm 0.57, IH with kindness −0.03, IB with
kindness 0.47, IB with pointless harm 0.35.

| direction, strength | IB | IH | neutral | harm, no benefit | kindness |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| unsteered | 5.25 | 2.85 | 4.53 | 1.55 | 6.30 |
| IH, 4 | 5.18 | 4.53 | 4.74 | 3.47 | 5.46 |
| IH, 8 | 5.11 | 4.89 | 4.87 | 4.20 | 5.22 |
| IH minus harm, 4 | 5.22 | 3.86 | 4.57 | 2.48 | 5.90 |
| IH minus harm, 8 | 5.30 | 4.50 | 4.75 | 3.20 | 5.72 |
| IH minus harm and kindness, 4 | 5.32 | 3.98 | 4.73 | 2.52 | 5.95 |
| IH minus harm and kindness, 8 | 5.34 | 4.77 | 4.85 | 3.50 | 5.82 |
| IB minus kindness, −8 | 3.79 | 1.46 | 3.92 | 1.08 | 5.61 |
| IB minus kindness −8, IH minus harm 4 | 4.50 | 2.51 | 4.38 | 1.55 | 5.55 |

- **Projection does not separate them.** Per point of IH gained, the pointless-harm
  control rises by 1.1 with the raw direction (strength 4: IH +1.7, harm +1.9) and by 0.9
  to 1.0 with the cleaned one (strength 4: +1.0 and +0.9; strength 8: +1.7 and +1.6).
  Removing the component along "accept pointless harm" removes a third of the IH gain and
  the same third of the harm gain. The two move together whatever is projected out.
- **The IB side is different.** IB with kindness projected out, at −8, lowers IB by 1.5
  while kindness drops 0.7 instead of 1.4; but IH drops by 1.4 with it, so the cleaned IB
  direction is a worse IB direction. The combined cleaned setting lands at (4.5, 2.5),
  far from the lay population that the raw setting reached.

## Reading

In this model, the representation that makes an answer accept instrumental harm is the
one that makes it accept harm: there is no linear component of the IH direction that
moves the scale while leaving the pointless-harm control alone. The IH axis is a harm
acceptance axis. The scale and the control are still distinct in the unsteered model
(IH 2.85, pointless harm 1.55: the model distinguishes them in its answers), so the
distinction is made somewhere the mean activation of the answer does not see, or by a
mechanism that is not a direction at layer 23. The raw directions stay the default for the
next models, with the harm control reported alongside every IH result.
